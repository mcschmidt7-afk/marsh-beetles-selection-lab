Which pattern blends in?

Test how inherited patterns can become more or less common when a marsh habitat changes.

A deliberately small model: each beetle has one of two inherited patterns; a visually hunting finch detects contrasting patterns more often; survivors reproduce. Rare inherited pattern variation can occur in offspring, never because a beetle needs it. Individuals never change pattern during their lifetime. Real populations have more variation and ecological pressures.
